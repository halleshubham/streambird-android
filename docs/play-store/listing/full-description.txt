StreamBird lets you go live from your phone to YouTube, Facebook and Twitch at the same time, with your own branding on the picture.

Open the app, point the camera, tick the channels you want and tap Go live. StreamBird sends one stream to our servers and relays it to every destination you chose.

REQUIRES A STREAMBIRD ACCOUNT
Sign in with your email and a one-time code. If you do not have an account yet, register on the StreamBird website. Channels (YouTube, Facebook Pages, Twitch) are connected on the website, then they appear in the app. Features and limits depend on your StreamBird plan.

GO LIVE IN ONE TAP
- Camera-first screen: the camera fills the screen, you add a title, pick your channels and press Go live.
- Saved presets: keep your usual setup (channels, brand kit, quality, ticker) as a card on the home screen and start it again with one tap.
- Portrait (9:16) and landscape (16:9) streams, with 540p, 720p or 1080p quality depending on your plan.
- Front or back camera, flip while you stream.

YOUR BRAND ON EVERY STREAM
- Brand kits: a logo with position and size, separate sets for portrait and landscape.
- Scrolling news ticker with your own text, changed live while you stream.
- Your own ads on the stream (picture or animated GIF): lower third, left third or full screen, always shown or on a schedule. Ads and brand kits are available on paid plans.
- The Free plan includes a small StreamBird mark and a StreamBird banner on the picture.

STAY ON AIR
- The stream keeps running with the screen off, with a notification while you are live.
- If your connection drops or you switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data, the app reconnects to the same stream automatically.
- A send-quality indicator tells you when your connection or phone is limiting the picture.
- Set a thumbnail for your stream before you go live.
- Share your permanent live link with one tap.
- If the app ever closes unexpectedly, you can share a crash report with us if you choose to.

THINGS TO KNOW
- You need a steady upload connection. About 3 Mbps is recommended for 720p.
- Streaming uses your camera and microphone. The app asks for permission and shows a notification while you are live.
- StreamBird does not record or store your stream.
- Your channels' own rules still apply: for example, YouTube needs live streaming enabled on your channel.
- The app does not sell anything inside the app and shows no third-party ads. The only ads are the ones you choose to put on your own stream (or the StreamBird banner on the Free plan).
